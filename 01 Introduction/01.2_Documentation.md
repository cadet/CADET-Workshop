**CADET-Core:** The heart of CADET: A fast and accurate solver for many models e.g. chromatography, filtration, crystallization and fermentation, written in C++.

* **Repository:** https://github.com/cadet/CADET-Core/

* **Documentation:** https://cadet.github.io/master/index.html
